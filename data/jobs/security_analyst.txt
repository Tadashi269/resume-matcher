Cybersecurity Analyst - Security Operations Centre

About the role:
Our Security Operations Centre protects the company's networks and cloud infrastructure 24x7. As a Security Analyst you will detect, investigate and respond to threats.

Responsibilities:
- Monitor security alerts in our SIEM (Splunk) and triage suspicious activity
- Lead incident response for phishing, malware and intrusion incidents
- Perform vulnerability assessment using tools like Nessus and track remediation
- Analyse network traffic with Wireshark to investigate anomalies
- Maintain firewall and IDS/IPS rules and contribute to threat hunting using MITRE ATT&CK

Requirements:
- 1+ years of experience in a SOC or security operations role
- Strong understanding of network security, TCP/IP, DNS and firewalls
- Hands-on experience with a SIEM such as Splunk or QRadar
- Knowledge of incident response procedures
- Scripting in Python or Bash for automation
- Familiarity with Linux

Nice to have:
- Certifications like CompTIA Security+, CEH or CCNA
- Experience with penetration testing tools such as Burp Suite or Metasploit
- Knowledge of compliance frameworks such as ISO 27001 or NIST
